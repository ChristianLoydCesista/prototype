About the Community Intelligence System

Official Platform

System Overview

The Arteche Community Intelligence System (CIS) is a comprehensive data platform engineered to streamline local governance and public services across all 27 barangays of the Municipality of Arteche, Eastern Samar.

Our Mission

To empower municipal and barangay leadership with high-fidelity, data-driven insights, maximize resource optimization, accelerate document service execution, and lower vulnerabilities via precise poverty targeting.

Core Architecture & Capabilities

Household Management
Comprehensive structural survey metrics with integrated vulnerability algorithms.
Advanced GIS Visualization
Dynamic spatial heatmaps indicating real-time resource allocations and risk trends.
Digital Certificate Handling
Paperless filing pipelines for fast-track processing of clearances and local indigency papers.
Risk Analysis Protocols
Automated mathematical scaling flags highly critical situations for instant state aid.
Consolidated Framework
Unified database ecosystems syncing performance indices across all 27 distinct sectors.
Citizen-Centric Accessibility
Accessible layouts designed to bridge localized infrastructure networks seamlessly.
Statutory Privacy Framework Compliance

This system operates in absolute conformity with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines. Protected metrics logged under census workflows are gathered exclusively for legitimate government profiling, hazard identification, and responsive social infrastructure enhancements.
